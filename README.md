# Fitness Subscription Analytics

This is a subscription analytic report for FitnessHub, a subscription-based fitness platform that offers personalized workout plans, nutrition coaching, and on-demand fitness classes.

Over the past two years, FitnessHub has experienced rapid customer growth, more than doubling its December YoY new subscribers. However, many customers typically cancel their subscriptions early on; with the sharpest decline occurs around months two and three, after which retention stabilizes after with only a few cancelations per month.

FitnessHub has demonstrated strong upward growth, starting under $25,000 in early 2024, climbing to $98,086 by late 2024, and peaking at $275,624 in late 2025. There are two noticeable seasonal patterns: revenue spikes during the holiday season and dips in January and February. FitnessHub is projected to post continued revenue growth over the next year. We expect revenue to be between $290,297 and approximately $400,000, depending on how strong the holiday season is.

FitnessHub generated a total Lifetime Value (LTV) of $2,594,595, with $1,210,721 captured in 2024 and $1,384,238 captured in 2025. However, cost scaled alongside value; the average total CAC increased from $270,321 in 2024 to $409,381 in 2025. Because of this increase, the time required to recover Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) increased between cohorts. While the 2024 cohorts recovered their CAC in just over 3 months,  the 2025 cohorts took longer, requiring just an over 5 months.

In 2025 FitnessHub achieved a Lifetime Value to Customer Acquisition Cost (LTV:CAC) ratio of 3.38:1. This indicates healthy unit economics and ready for increased investment in growth. Therefore, we recommend continuing to scale marketing efforts to acquire more customers.
